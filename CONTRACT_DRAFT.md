## 1. BACKGROUND
The Client desires to have a professional website developed for their used car dealership ("Pacific Cars and Trucks" branding or similar). The Developer agrees to design, develop, and deploy said website. In lieu of cash payment, the Client agrees to compensate the Developer with a specific motor vehicle.

## 2. SCOPE OF SERVICES (THE "DELIVERABLES")
The Developer agrees to provide the following services:

1.  **Frontend Development:** Creation of a responsive, mobile-friendly React-based website including Home, Inventory, Vehicle Details, Financing, Trade-In, About, and Contact pages.
2.  **Design Customization:** Implementation of brand colors (#1E63FF, #002B60), "AI" styling, and custom animations as demonstrated in the prototype.
3.  **Backend CMS Development:** Implementation of a Content Management System (CMS) (e.g., Strapi, Sanity, or Firebase) to allow the Client to easily add, edit, and remove vehicle inventory without coding.
4.  **Training:** Provision of training (video call or recorded walkthrough) on how to use the CMS to manage inventory.
5.  **Deployment:** Setup of hosting (e.g., Vercel, Netlify, or Client's preferred host) and domain name configuration.
6.  **30-Day Support Period:** For a period of thirty (30) days following the Launch Date, the Developer will provide maintenance, bug fixes, and minor customizations/changes requested by the Client at no additional cost.

## 4. TIMELINE AND LAUNCH
*   **Deadline:** The Developer guarantees the website will be live, fully functional, and publicly accessible via the domain name no later than **fourteen (14) days** after the signing of this Agreement (the "Deadline").
*   **Launch:** "Launch" is defined as the website being deployed to the production URL with the CMS connected and functioning.

## 5. OBLIGATIONS
*   **Developer:** Responsible for all coding, design implementation, CMS setup, and ensuring the site meets modern web standards (speed, accessibility).
*   **Client:** Responsible for providing high-quality images of inventory, dealership logo files, and purchasing the domain name (URL) and hosting subscription (if applicable).

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
The Developer hereby assigns to the Client all right, title, and interest in the custom code, design, and content of the website, effective immediately. This assignment covers the Work in its current state ("As-Is") and includes all future modifications and the final version of the website ("Final Deliverable"). The Developer retains the right to use the work in their professional portfolio.

## 7. TERMINATION AND FAILURE TO DELIVER
*   **By Developer (Failure to Deliver):** If the Developer fails to deliver the fully functional website by the Deadline (14 days), the Developer is in breach of contract. In such an event, the Developer must **immediately return the Exchange Vehicle** to the Client in the same condition as received (normal wear and tear excepted) OR pay the Client the Agreed Value ($[Dollar Amount]) in cash within 5 business days.
*   **By Client (Failure to Transfer):** If the Client fails to transfer the title and possession of the vehicle upon the signing of this Agreement, the Developer is under no obligation to commence work and this Agreement is void.

## 8. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
The Developer is an independent contractor, not an employee of the Client. The Developer is responsible for their own taxes regarding the value of the Exchange Vehicle.

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements.
